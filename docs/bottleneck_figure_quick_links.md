- [Procedure-group workload and scheduling performance](bottleneck_figures/procedure_group_workload_status.png) - Section 10.11.7. Shows procedure groups with different workload and scheduling outcomes.
- [Procedure group by start hour](bottleneck_figures/procedure_group_start_hour_status.png) - Section 10.11.7. Shows whether procedure-group overrun patterns change by start-hour band.
- [Time-of-day overrun and underrun profile](bottleneck_figures/time_of_day_overrun_profile.png) - Section 10.11.8. Shows higher overrun pressure in some hours and more underrun in others. Start hour is provisional.
- [Executive bottleneck evidence dashboard](bottleneck_figures/executive_bottleneck_dashboard.png) - Section 10.14.1. Combines volume, overrun rate, total overrun minutes, median error, and occupancy for prioritisation.
